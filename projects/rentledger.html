RentRent Ledger.Ledger.

Desktop app for managing rental estates. Record a payment once and it settles the right months, issues the receipt, and updates every balance, from security deposits to waived rent.

About this project

My mom looks after her rentals around a full schedule: her job, our family, and the admin she runs at her office and at church. Every payment meant a handwritten receipt, then an entry in an Excel file with a tab for each estate. Lump sums had to be split across the months they settled by hand, and finding out who still owed what meant going through every sheet.

RentLedger lets her stay on top of her rentals without the bookkeeping taking over. She can see who owes what at a glance, and tenants get a clear record of the months they’ve paid. For me, it was a chance to learn Electron: GitHub Actions tests and builds it into a Windows installer that updates without touching her data, and the same code could ship for Mac too.
