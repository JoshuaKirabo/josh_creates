About this project

This project came from genuine frustration and exhaustion. Having ADHD, deciding what to do, in what order, and for how long was one of the most paralyzing parts of my day. That was the work, before any of the actual work, and a lot of days I never got to the stuff I meant to do.

I wanted something that could plan my days with me. With NextStep I just talk: it gathers the context from what I say, keeps it in mind, and helps me turn it into a schedule I can actually follow.
