Clients upload spreadsheets, documents, images and paper records through a client portal connected to SurveyCTO. SurveyCTO exports the collected records in a common CSV format. The pipeline cleans those records and loads them into Azure SQL for reporting.

About this project

During my internship at Tellistic, we were entering client records by hand and wanted to automate some of that work. Some clients had older records which were still on paper, and even the digital files varied from client to client.

I had never done this before, so I opened up VS Code, tried a few things in Python, and went looking for answers on YouTube. I put together an idea for a pipeline and took it to my manager. He liked it and told me to go ahead. I learned my way around Azure as I built it. In the end, we could take data from the client portal and get a clean CSV out of it. That saved us time on data entry, and we could use the same setup the next time we ran into this problem.
