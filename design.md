# Mobile Meet Josh

## Direction and scope

Refine the existing personal, monochrome introduction at widths up to 700px.
The childhood photograph, grain, Bounded heading, Google Sans body, and page
transitions are the references. Preserve their character rather than introducing
a different visual system. Desktop keeps its existing composition.

## Reading and hierarchy

The photograph and “Meet Josh.” establish the personal story first. Give the
opening sentence emphasis and split the original story into readable paragraphs.
Keep every word. Left-align the mobile title and prose on the same reading edge.
Use 16px body text with a 1.6 line height and 24px paragraph spacing.

Meet Josh is one fixed screen on a phone, and the story reads a part at a time
(Josh, 2026-11-21): the opening line with the first paragraph, then the second
paragraph, then the third. A flick swaps the words in place while the photograph,
heading and actions hold still. A fourth flick turns to Experience, and flicking
up walks back through the parts before folding to Home. The three parts share one
box as tall as the longest, centred between the heading and the actions. Each
change dissolves line by line, as a project page's story does when it leaves. The two actions keep
their place but stay hidden until the last part, then rise in as its last line
comes clear. Preserve every word. Phones under 520px tall and
reduced motion keep the story in natural page flow.

## Layout and actions

Show the photograph full width. It gives up height so the screen fits, cropping
from the foot first to keep the face and computer. Fade the top 18% and bottom 12% into the grain.
The left-aligned heading overlaps the photograph by 32px, or 56px on phones up to
600px tall.
Keep 32px between the heading, story and actions. Remove mobile footer reservation.
Keep at least 32px or the device safe area below the buttons.
Use an outlined résumé action and a filled contact action, with simple trailing
icons, 48px minimum targets, and the existing immediate press/focus feedback.
Do not change the grain, add colors, introduce dependencies, or invent content.

## Verification

Inspect 320px, 375px, and 390px phone widths, a short landscape viewport, and
desktop. Check photo-edge fades, heading overlap, action navigation, full story
access, image crop, overflow, and the existing fold/section tests. Keep desktop
paragraph spacing and button treatment intact.
